# Visual Focus Notes

These are the current creative constraints for Croonies: Treasure Troop icons and store-facing UI.

## App Icon Direction

- Character-led icons perform best in casual, adventure, and family game categories.
- Faces and expressive characters should be prioritized because they drive higher click-through rates.
- High contrast is critical. Icons must read clearly at tiny sizes, including app-store search sizes around `60x60` px.
- Gradients and subtle glows are current-market friendly and should be used intentionally.
- Soft 3D is the preferred finish: premium, tactile, friendly, and not overly noisy.
- Avoid clutter. Strong icons should usually contain only `1-3` major elements.

## Croonies-Specific Takeaway

Best icon candidates should focus on one expressive Croonie face or a small group of kids, plus one treasure cue such as a glowing map, flashlight, coin, or chest. Do not overcrowd with full scenes, many props, or tiny text.
